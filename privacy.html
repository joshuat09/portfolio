PRIVACY

Privacy.

This is a personal portfolio. It collects very little, sets no tracking cookies and has no accounts or adverts. Last updated 3 November 2026.

What is collected

  • Page counts. I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to see how many people visit which pages. It does not use cookies and does not build a profile of you. It records the page, the referring site, your browser and screen size, and your country. It does not store your IP address.
  • The contact form. If you use it, the name, email address and message you type are sent through Web3Forms to my email inbox, and I use them only to reply to you. The form is optional; you can email me directly instead.
  • Fonts. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads. That includes your IP address, as with any web request.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

What stays in your browser

  • Your light or dark mode choice and your cursor effect setting are saved in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to me and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.
  • The demos run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent anywhere.

Your choices

  • Browser privacy settings and most content blockers will stop the analytics script from loading, and the site works the same without it.
  • To ask me to delete a message you sent, email joshuatait09@gmail.com.
Design principle: collect the least data that does the job.
