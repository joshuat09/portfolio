PROTOTYPE · INTERACTIVE DEMO

Graduate job finder.

Most job recommenders hide why a role was suggested. This prototype shows its working: edit a candidate profile and every role gets a 0–100 score you can trace back to skills, location, salary, sector and level.

  1. Profile
  2. Weights
  3. Component scores
  4. 0–100 match

This is a prototype demo. The roles and companies are fictional and the scoring uses fixed rules. It runs entirely in your browser: no AI model, no network calls, nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
