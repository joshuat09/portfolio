Safety invariants

Rules that must hold across every scenario, checked on every page load.

    How to read this

    Expected outcome
    Supported: automation may resolve it. Blocked: automation must not act, so a human decides. Unresolved: not enough verified information to act.
    Actual gate result
    Allow: the safety gate let a resolution through. Block: the gate stopped a proposed resolution. Unknown: the case never reached the gate.
    Safe divergence
    The outcome is right but the route differs from the contract. Here, billing, refund and service complaints are human-only by policy, so they are routed to a person before the gate is ever consulted. The contract expects the gate to block them, which is a useful gap to surface and decide on.
    Unsafe
    The agent acted where the contract requires a human. The target is always zero.
    Design principle: evaluate against a written contract, not against the agent's own behaviour. Where the two disagree, the disagreement is the finding.
