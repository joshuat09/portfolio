    How to read this

    Expected classification
    What the contract says should happen. Supported: the agent may act within scope. Blocked: it must not. Unresolved: the contract leaves it open, so the scenario is not scored right or wrong.
    Gate outcome
    What the deterministic gate actually decided. Allow, block, or unknown when the evidence is too weak to decide either way.
    Evidence states
    Evidence categories
    Design principle: write down what should happen, then check what the controls actually did. Where the two disagree, or the contract is silent, that is the finding.
