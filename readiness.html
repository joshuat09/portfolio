AI readiness scorecard.

Should a business process be handled by people, simple rules, AI assistance or an agent? Describe the process in ten answers and the scorecard recommends the least autonomy that does the job, and shows its reasoning.

A thinking aid, not research. It applies ideas from my dissertation (the resource-based view, socio-technical systems and agency theory) to a few simple rules. The thresholds are my own judgement and are all shown below, so use it to start a discussion, not to make a decision. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

    2 · Why: the numbers

    The rules behind the scores
    • Rule fit = half "steps are predictable" + half "inputs are uniform".
    • Agent fit = 60% "inputs are messy" + 40% "volume is high".
    • Risk = 50% impact + 30% irreversibility + 20% accountability.
    • Readiness = 30% data + 25% skills + 30% governance + 15% people.
    • Rules are recommended when rule fit is 65+ and risk is under 75.
    • An agent needs agent fit 50+, readiness 55+, governance 3+ and risk under 65.
    • Gates: serious and hard-to-undo harm, or strict accountability with weak governance, cap autonomy at "AI-assisted".
    • The least autonomous option that passes is the one recommended.
